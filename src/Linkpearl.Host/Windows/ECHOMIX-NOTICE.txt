EchoMix in Linkpearl
====================

The Music profile MIX button hosts EchoMix by jfraygit
(https://github.com/jfraygit/EchoXIV, EchoMix/), used with the author's
permission. Source lives under vendor/EchoMix and is compiled into
Linkpearl.EchoMix plus EchoMix.AudioHost.exe.

EchoMix is licensed under GNU AGPL-3.0-or-later. Linkpearl is also
AGPL-3.0-or-later.

Going live from EchoMix lists the DJ on Pearlgate / Icecast (the Linkpearl
station). DJs who prefer outside gear can still capture that mix from
Music's DJ dashboard instead of mixing inside EchoMix.
